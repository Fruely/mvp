# Freuly Market Radar

## Purpose

Market Radar is the data and automation layer that helps Freuly understand where real market activity exists.

It collects and classifies public market signals:

- supply signals: specialists offer services;
- demand signals: people search for services.

The goal is not to collect random contacts.  
The goal is to understand categories, cities, channels, demand, supply and growth opportunities.

---

## Core Principle

Freuly should not guess the market.

Freuly should observe public signals, classify them, aggregate them and then decide:

- which categories to create;
- which subcategories to prioritize;
- which cities and regions to target;
- which SEO pages to build;
- which Threads/Telegram content to publish;
- which specialists to scout;
- where to spend advertising money.

---

## Signal Types

### Supply Signal

A supply signal means that a specialist publicly presents themselves or offers a service.

Examples:

- "Маникюр Berlin"
- "Электрик Köln"
- "Фотограф München"
- "Психолог онлайн"
- "Ремонт квартир Düsseldorf"
- "Репетитор немецкого для детей"

Supply signals help Freuly understand where specialists already exist.

---

### Demand Signal

A demand signal means that a potential client is looking for a service.

Examples:

- "Ищу русскоязычного электрика в Köln"
- "Посоветуйте мастера маникюра в Berlin"
- "Нужен психолог онлайн"
- "Кто делает ремонт ванной в Düsseldorf?"
- "Ищу репетитора немецкого для ребёнка"

Demand signals help Freuly understand where clients already need help.

---
