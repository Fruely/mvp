# Third-Party Data Attribution

## GeoNames postal-code data

Freuly uses German postal-code data derived from the GeoNames postal-code dataset.

- **Source:** GeoNames — https://www.geonames.org/
- **Dataset:** https://download.geonames.org/export/zip/DE.zip
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- **License text:** https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The source data was transformed and deterministically deduplicated by Freuly
to produce one approximate reference coordinate per postal code.

The coordinates are PLZ-level approximations. They are not exact street-address
or business-location coordinates.

GeoNames does not sponsor, endorse, or otherwise approve Freuly.
